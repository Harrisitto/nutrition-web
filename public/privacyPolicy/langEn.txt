Welcome to Nutrition App. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.
By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
We collect minimal information to provide you with the best possible service:
Information You Provide Directly
-Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address only. This is required to identify you and link you to your nutritionist.
-Profile Information: You may choose to provide additional information such as your weight, sex, height, dietary preferences...
- Communications: If you contact us for support, we may receive your email address and the content of your message.
To improve app performance and user experience, certain data (such as app settings and preferences) is cached locally on your device. This information never leaves your phone unless you actively sync it.
We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account.
- Enable you to log your meals and track nutrition.
- Allow your assigned nutritionist to view your progress and provide professional guidance.
- Communicate with you about app updates or respond to your support requests.
- Improve and optimize the App's functionality.
We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.
The unique feature of Nutrition App is the collaboration between you and your professional nutritionist.
- Access to Data: Your nutritionist has their own dedicated account. They can access the database to view the information you have entered (such as your food logs and optional metrics).
- Purpose: This access is strictly limited to providing you with personalized dietary advice and monitoring your progress.
- Confidentiality: Your nutritionist is bound by professional confidentiality obligations regarding your data.

- Database Location: Your account information and any data you enter is securely stored on servers hosted by Supabase in France (Europe). This ensures your data is protected under strict European data protection laws (GDPR).
- Security Measures: We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data in transit and at rest.
- Local Cache: Data stored temporarily in your phone's cache is cleared automatically within 15 to 30 minutes.

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third parties.
The only exception is when we are required to do so by law (for example, in response to a court order or legal process).

We believe in your right to control your data.
- Account Deletion: If you choose to delete your account:
    - Your data is **immediately and permanently deleted** from our database (Supabase).
    - Cached data remaining on your phone will be cleared automatically within **15 to 30 minutes**.
- Inactivity: We do not retain your data longer than necessary. Data is kept only as long as your account is active.

Depending on your location, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:
- The Right to Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- The Right to Rectification: You can correct any inaccurate information. (You can also update your optional metrics directly in the app).
- The Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can request that we delete your data. (You can also do this immediately by deleting your account).
- The Right to Data Portability:*You can request a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used format.
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

Our App is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
